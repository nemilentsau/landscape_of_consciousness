# Course Context For Group 002

Group 002 is the second episode in the top-level orientation sequence. It should continue the opening frame from group 001 without redoing that episode.

## Already Covered In Group 001

- Chalmers's hard problem as the central challenge for phenomenal consciousness.
- The distinction between phenomenal consciousness, access consciousness, reportability, metacognition, wakefulness, and other uses of "consciousness."
- The epistemology-versus-ontology and correlation-versus-explanation cautions that apply to every later theory.
- The role of surveys, typologies, and theory maps in organizing a field with many incompatible explanatory targets.
- The broad worldview frame: physicalism/materialism, dualism, idealism, monism, and other nonphysicalist alternatives.

## Scope For Group 002

Focus on Kuhn sections 6-10:

- Section 6: whether consciousness is primitive or fundamental.
- Section 7: identity theory.
- Section 8: Kuhn's landscape framing.
- Section 9: materialism theories as a family.
- Section 10: non-reductive physicalism.

## Production Constraint

Use group 001 only as course grounding. Do not re-research the hard problem, Mary, zombies, broad survey taxonomy, or the general physicalism-versus-nonphysicalism setup except where they are needed to explain sections 6-10. The episode should feel like the next step in the course: from "what must be explained?" to "what major metaphysical and physicalist options are now on the table?"
